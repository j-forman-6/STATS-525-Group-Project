# Project Log
 
A running record of **what we did, who did it, and what's still open**.
Add a new entry at the **top** every time you work on the project, then commit and push.
The instructor may check contributions, so be specific: name the files you touched and the results you found.
 
---
 
## How to write an entry
 
```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD: Your Name
**Did:**
- What you worked on (and which file: R/02_eda.R, report.qmd, ...)
 
**Found:**
- Results, numbers, or observations. Math is fine: $\hat\beta_1 = 2.31,\ p = 0.004$
 
**Questions / next steps:**
- Anything unresolved, or what should happen next
```
